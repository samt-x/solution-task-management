## The aggregate board and the portfolio board are two different things

The aggregate board collects **tasks** from all boards. The portfolio board in the track [Portfolio management](../portefoljestyring/) has one card per **pilot, product or work area**. They are kept separate.

## Personal board: reads, but does not write back

An issue can be on several boards at once. Closing, assignees and comments belong to the issue itself and are therefore shared by all boards. Status, priority and deadlines belong to the board and are owned by it.

The personal board therefore reads from the project boards, but never writes back to them. The project's priority and my own priority across projects are two different and equally valid questions. The reasoning is in [solution-task-management#5](https://github.com/samt-x/solution-task-management/issues/5).
